## Documentation Principle

Technical claims must be supported by:

* Implementation evidence.
* Experimental evidence.
* Appropriate references.

Do not make unsupported claims.

---

## Important Terminology

Use consistently:

**Energy Optimization Opportunity**

rather than automatically calling every detected pattern an:

**Energy Smell**

Use:

**Predicted Impact**

for model/rule predictions.

Use:

**Measured Impact**

for experimentally observed results.

Use:

**Validated Optimization**

only after correctness and empirical evaluation.

---

## Academic Integrity

Do not fabricate:

* Results.
* Energy savings.
* Accuracy.
* Experimental measurements.
* References.

Clearly distinguish:

* Existing research.
* Proposed methodology.
* Implemented functionality.
* Experimental findings.
* Future work.
